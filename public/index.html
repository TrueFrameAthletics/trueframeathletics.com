Built for Athletes Who Want More Than Motivation

TrueFrame Athletics works with developing athletes who want to become more confident, composed, resilient, and consistent when competition becomes difficult.

We help athletes who may:

  • Play well in practice but struggle under game pressure
  • Lose confidence after mistakes
  • Become hesitant after turnovers, misses, errors, or setbacks
  • Have difficulty resetting and moving to the next play
  • Overthink during competition
  • Struggle to trust their training
  • Become visibly frustrated when things go wrong
  • Need better focus and composure in important moments
  • Want to understand what their competition film is really showing

TrueFrame is designed for athletes across sports, including basketball, football, volleyball, soccer, baseball, softball, wrestling, track and field, and other competitive sports.

We Don't Just Talk About the Mental Game. We Look for It in Competition.

Confidence, composure, focus, and resilience matter — but athletes need more than conversations about mindset.

TrueFrame looks at what actually happens when the athlete competes.

Competition film can reveal moments such as:

  • What happens immediately after a mistake
  • Hesitation before an important decision
  • Changes in body language
  • Reduced aggression after failure
  • Communication after adversity
  • How quickly an athlete re-engages
  • Decision-making under pressure
  • Whether frustration affects the next play
  • Whether the athlete trusts their training when the moment becomes difficult

Those observations become part of the athlete's development plan. Then we return to competition and film to evaluate whether those behaviors are changing.

The TrueFrame Performance Loop

Mental performance becomes more valuable when athletes can connect what they learn to what they actually do during competition.

1. Baseline

Understand the athlete, their goals, strengths, challenges, confidence patterns, pressure responses, and current performance habits.

2. Film

Review competition film for observable performance behaviors, including mistakes, hesitation, decision-making, body language, composure, communication, and response to adversity.

3. Train

Develop specific mental-performance strategies, reset routines, cues, behaviors, and sport-specific applications.

4. Compete

The athlete applies those tools in practice and competition when pressure, mistakes, and adversity actually occur.

5. Verify

Return to competition film and performance observations to evaluate what changed, what improved, and what needs continued development.

The FRAME Reset Method

Competition will never be perfect. The goal is not to eliminate difficult moments. The goal is to develop a better response to them.

F

Find It

Recognize what happened without allowing the moment to control what happens next.

R

Reset

Interrupt the negative emotional or mental response.

A

Anchor

Return attention to a controllable cue, action, breath, word, assignment, or responsibility.

M

Move

Physically and mentally re-engage with the competition.

E

Execute

Make the next decision with commitment.

The FRAME Reset Method gives athletes a repeatable process for responding when competition gets hard.

What We Look For

TrueFrame observes coaching and performance markers—not psychological diagnoses.

Reset Speed

How quickly does the athlete mentally and behaviorally recover after a mistake?

Response to Adversity

Does a difficult moment affect the next play, possession, rep, or decision?

Decision Trust

Does the athlete recognize an opportunity and commit to the decision, or hesitate?

Competitive Composure

How does the athlete respond when pressure increases?

Body-Language Recovery

What happens physically after mistakes, setbacks, or frustration?

Re-engagement

How quickly does the athlete return to productive competition?

Communication

Does communication disappear or improve when the athlete experiences adversity?

Focus

Can the athlete return attention to the present play instead of remaining stuck on the previous one?

Execution Under Pressure

Can the athlete apply their training when the situation becomes more difficult?

TrueFrame does not promise perfect performances. We help athletes develop better responses and more consistent performance behaviors.

TrueFrame adapts mental-performance development to the athlete's sport rather than treating every athlete exactly the same.
