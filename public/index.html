TrueFrame Athletics — Mental Performance & Adversity Response

We help athletes develop repeatable responses to mistakes, pressure, adversity, confidence loss, role changes and high-stakes competition. Our FRAME system gives athletes practical tools they can use in competition rather than relying only on motivational messaging.

Built for Athletes Who Want More Than Motivation

TrueFrame Athletics works with developing athletes who want to become more confident, composed, resilient, and consistent when competition becomes difficult.

We help athletes who may:

  • Play well in practice but struggle under game pressure
  • Lose confidence after mistakes
  • Become hesitant after turnovers, misses, errors, or setbacks
  • Have difficulty resetting and moving to the next play
  • Overthink during competition
  • Struggle to trust their training
  • Become visibly frustrated when things go wrong
  • Need better focus and composure in important moments
  • Want to understand what their competition film is really showing

We Don't Just Talk About the Mental Game. We Look for It in Competition.

Confidence, composure, focus, and resilience matter — but athletes need more than conversations about mindset.

TrueFrame looks at what actually happens when the athlete competes.

Competition film can reveal moments such as:

  • What happens immediately after a mistake
  • Hesitation before an important decision
  • Changes in body language
  • Reduced aggression after failure
  • Communication after adversity
  • How quickly an athlete re-engages
  • Decision-making under pressure
  • Whether frustration affects the next play
  • Whether the athlete trusts their training when the moment becomes difficult
