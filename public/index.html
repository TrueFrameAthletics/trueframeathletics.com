MENTAL SKILLS IN COMPETITION

Mental toughness you can practice.
Support you can use during the game.

TrueFrame Athletics is based in Omaha, Nebraska. Richard helps athletes practice focus, composure, communication, and responses to mistakes through the FRAME Reset Method. Real-time coaching reinforces those skills while pressure and frustration are still present.

For TrueFrame, mental toughness means returning to a useful action when competition gets difficult. The work connects a familiar reset cue to the coach’s next instruction.

In-person practice and sideline support are arranged with each program. Location, travel, availability, and access are confirmed before scheduling.

FRAME — the reset your athletes use in the moment.

Find It — notice what's actually happening: the mistake, the frustration, the distraction.

Reset — one breath, one cue. Let the last play go.

Anchor — lock onto the next assignment, not the last play.

Move — get back into position, communicate, be ready.

Execute — do the next job, all the way through.
