IN THE MOMENT

Across sports. Specific to the moment.

A missed free throw. A dropped pass. A missed serve. A strikeout. The setting changes; the work remains helping an athlete return attention to their next responsibility. Support may take place on the bench, sideline, dugout, or another approved team area.

IN THE MOMENT

Start with a focused pilot.

Choose a team, define the behaviors you want to observe, and agree on a manageable set of practices and approved competition opportunities. Scope, availability, travel, and fees are discussed before scheduling.
