SIDELINE SUPPORT / PRACTICE INTEGRATION / ALL SPORTS

High School Teams

I help high school athletes reset after mistakes so one bad play doesn’t turn into three. We practice a short reset with your team, then, with your program’s approval, I reinforce it on the sideline while the game is still happening.

See what happens in the free team pilot →

College Teams

I help college athletes reset after mistakes and get back to the assignment their coach gave them. We rehearse short reset cues in practice. When your program approves sideline access, I reinforce those same cues during competition, while the pressure is still real.

Start with one team and one pressure moment

Pick a situation your staff keeps seeing, like a turnover that carries into the next defensive possession or a missed serve that changes how an athlete approaches the next point. If your staff wants to see whether the response carries over, we can discuss working across several practices.

FOR COACHES AND ATHLETIC STAFF

Working With Your Staff

Your coaches own the game plan. Before I step into an approved team area, we agree on when I can check in, what language fits your program, and what you want to hear afterward.

Timing

Agree on sideline access, where I’ll stand, and which breaks in practice or competition are appropriate.

Language

Use cues that fit your staff’s instruction, then rehearse them with athletes before game day.

Communication

Decide who gets feedback and what patterns to bring back to practice. I work alongside your coaches, athlete development staff, and sports psychology team.
