ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE

A short note with a practical next step.

Situation
Player comes to the bench after two turnovers and stops communicating.
Check-in
At an agreed break, Richard reinforces a practiced FRAME cue and asks what the coach needs next.
Chosen action
Call the defensive assignment when returning to play.
Observation
In this fictional example, the player calls the assignment on the next possession. This single observation does not establish lasting change.
Follow-up
Ask the athlete what helped; rehearse the cue in the next pressure drill and observe it across further opportunities.

IN THE MOMENT

Track actions, not assumptions.

Useful notes separate what was observed from what the athlete reported. Communication, return to the assignment, and follow-through can guide coaching. No diagnosis or guaranteed outcome is implied.
