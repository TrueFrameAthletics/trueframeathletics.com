The problem

Coaches see it every season.

A team that executes in practice falls apart during a run by the opponent. A starter can’t let go of a turnover. Players tighten up in the playoffs. Coaches have limited practice time, and the mental side gets squeezed out.

TrueFrame gives your program that system: structured sessions, a common language for resets and composure, and film-based feedback that connects mental performance to what actually happens in competition.

Programs

Flexible formats for your program.

Team sessions

Mental Performance Sessions

Interactive team sessions on confidence, composure, focus, and responding to mistakes. Built for real game situations.

Season-long

Season Program

A structured program through preseason, conference play, and postseason, with sessions timed to the pressure points of the year.

Staff

Coach Workshops

Practical tools for coaches: how to talk to athletes after mistakes, build confidence during slumps, and create a stronger standard under pressure.

Film

Team Film & Pressure Review

We review team film for pressure moments: momentum swings, late-game decisions, and body language under stress.

How we work with programs
