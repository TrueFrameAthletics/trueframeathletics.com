The free pilot and how it works

What does it cost?
The pilot is free. If you want more after that, we talk about what fits your program. I'm not here to sell you anything today.
Is this therapy? Are you a counselor?
No. This is performance skills training — the mental side of the game, like a strength coach for how you respond to adversity. If an athlete needs counseling, that's your school counselor's lane, and I'd point them there.
When would this happen?
Thirty minutes, at a practice or before a game — whenever it costs your coach the least time. I work around your schedule.
How is this different from a sports psychologist?
My role is to help athletes reset in real time. When sideline access is arranged with your program, I work there alongside your staff, in the moment, while the game is still happening.
Who else have you worked with?
You can read feedback from athletes, parents, and coaches on my site. There’s also a short video showing what a reset can look like on the bench.
