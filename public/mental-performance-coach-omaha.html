Mental Performance Coaching in Omaha

Build a response you can practice. TrueFrame Athletics supports athletes, coaches, and teams with practical mental performance and adversity-response training.

Omaha-based coaching for the moments after a mistake

Richard Cooks, founder of TrueFrame Athletics, is based in Omaha, Nebraska. He helps athletes and coaches practice responses to mistakes, pressure, confidence setbacks, and role changes through the FRAME Reset Method.

For an Omaha-area athlete, the starting point might be hesitation after a missed shot or difficulty returning attention to the next point. For a team, it might be communication that drops when the score gets tight. Training is adapted to the sport and the athlete’s responsibilities.

Individual coaching, team workshops, and staff support

Individual athlete coaching focuses on a personal reset routine and next-action cue. Own the Moment introduces the approach to a team in a 45–60 minute workshop. Coach workshops and season support help staff reinforce those skills in practice and competition.

In-person availability and meeting arrangements are confirmed during your consultation. Virtual coaching and film review are also available. Contact Richard before arranging travel; this page does not list a public walk-in office.

What to bring to your first conversation

  • The athlete’s sport, level, role, and goals.
  • One recent example of a difficult pressure moment.
  • Your practice schedule and preferred session format.
  • Whether competition film is available and authorized to share.

Film is optional. Athlete reflection and coach observations can help identify a useful starting point. Fees, format, and follow-up are agreed upon before booking.

Meet your coach

Richard holds a master’s degree in Industrial-Organizational Psychology and a bachelor’s degree in Psychology. He brings experience in coaching, athlete development, and performance leadership. Read Richard’s background and view an illustrative performance report.

TrueFrame offers performance coaching and athlete development, not clinical diagnosis or mental-health treatment. Results are not guaranteed.
