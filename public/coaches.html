Common questions

Coaches FAQs

What do coach workshops cover?

Workshops provide practical tools for talking with athletes after mistakes, building confidence during slumps, and creating a stronger standard under pressure.

What is the FRAME Reset Method?

It is a repeatable process: Find It, Reset, Anchor, Move, and Execute. Athletes practice returning attention to a controllable cue and committing to the next action.

How do we get started?

Start with a consultation to discuss your roster, season, and goals and choose a format that fits your program.
