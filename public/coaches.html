SIDELINE SUPPORT / PRACTICE INTEGRATION / ALL SPORTS

IN THE MOMENT

“We already tell players to move on.”

Absolutely. TrueFrame adds a dedicated person helping athletes put that instruction into action while frustration is still present. Richard can work with the player on the bench while you manage substitutions, matchups, and the next possession.

The aim is a brief reset and one clear action connected to your instruction—not another set of tactical directions.

WORKING ALONGSIDE YOUR STAFF

Your instruction comes first.

01

Agree before the game

We establish access, athlete needs, staff language, and the moments when a brief check-in fits.

02

Support during the game

Richard focuses on frustration, attention, communication, and readiness at appropriate breaks while staff manage tactics and execution.

03

Reinforce the next response

We observe follow-through and bring useful patterns back to practice and staff conversations.
