IN THE MOMENT

Why real-time support?

“A coach still has a game to manage when an athlete comes to the bench frustrated. My role is to help that athlete reset and get ready to carry out the coach’s next instruction.”

Richard built TrueFrame around practical, repeatable responses to mistakes, pressure, adversity, and role changes. His focus is bringing that work into the environment where athletes need to use it.

IN THE MOMENT

A background in people and performance.

Richard holds a master’s degree in Industrial-Organizational Psychology and a bachelor’s degree in Psychology. His background includes healthcare performance leadership, coaching and athlete development, and supporting soldiers preparing for overseas deployment.

TrueFrame applies that focus on behavior and performance through practice, brief real-time support, and observation. Services are mental-performance coaching, not therapy or clinical treatment.
