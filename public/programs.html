Every program uses the same practical approach: find the moment, train the response, and apply it in competition. Takeaways below describe what each program is designed to build, not guaranteed results.

01 · Individual

Individual Athlete Mental-Performance Coaching

One-on-one coaching that helps an athlete build a practical response to mistakes, pressure, and setbacks in their own sport.

Designed for

  • Athletes in any sport who want a more reliable response to mistakes and pressure
  • Athletes whose confidence or focus drops after errors, misses, or setbacks
  • Families who want to support an athlete's development

Common challenges

  • Playing well in practice but struggling under game pressure
  • Losing confidence after mistakes
  • Hesitation after turnovers, misses, or errors
  • Difficulty resetting and moving to the next play

What happens in training

  • A conversation about the athlete's sport, role, goals, and hardest moments
  • Review of available competition film for observable habits after mistakes
  • Practice with the FRAME Reset Method using sport-specific cues
  • Realistic pressure scenarios and a next-play plan

Expected takeaways

  • A personal reset routine and cue
  • Clearer awareness of what happens after mistakes
  • A practice plan athletes and coaches can reinforce

Delivery: In person or virtual. Film review and coaching work well virtually.

02 · Workshop

Own the Moment Team Workshop — 45–60 minutes

A hands-on introductory workshop that turns the FRAME Reset Method into a next-play routine, not just a motivational speech.

Designed for

  • High school, college, and club teams
  • Coaches who want a focused introduction to FRAME
  • Athletic directors looking for a short, practical session that works alongside existing staff

Common challenges

  • Frustration and hesitation after mistakes
  • Communication that drops when things go wrong
  • Momentum swings and role changes
  • Athletes who know what to do but struggle to do it under pressure

Own the Moment — 45–60 minutes

0–10 minutesIdentify the pressure moment
10–25 minutesLearn and rehearse FRAME
25–45 minutesPractice realistic pressure scenarios
45–60 minutesCreate an individual or team next-play plan

Expected takeaways

  • A repeatable reset routine for mistakes and setbacks
  • A personal next-action cue athletes can practice
  • Shared language to support team composure and communication
  • A starting point for follow-up coaching or a longer program

Delivery: Built around your team. We discuss your sport, roster, and pressure moments first, and confirm the format before booking.

03 · Teams

Team Adversity-Response Development

Ongoing work with a team on how it responds, together, to mistakes, momentum swings, and difficult stretches of a season.

Designed for

  • High school, college, and club programs
  • Coaches who want shared language for resets and next-play communication
  • Teams that want to respond better as a group, not only as individuals

Common challenges

  • Frustration spreading after errors or turnovers
  • Communication that drops when the team is behind
  • Slow recovery after momentum swings
  • Role changes that affect confidence and focus

What happens in training

  • A planning conversation about the team's sport, roster, and pressure moments
  • Team sessions that teach and rehearse FRAME
  • Sport-specific pressure scenarios built around the team's own situations
  • Follow-up with coaches on what is being observed and what to reinforce

Expected takeaways

  • Shared team language for resets
  • A team next-play routine
  • Cues coaches can reinforce in practice
  • A plan for continued development

Delivery: Sessions run on your schedule, in person or virtual.

04 · Coaches

Coach Education and Team Communication Workshops

Workshops that help coaches and staff reinforce resets and communicate clearly when pressure rises.

Designed for

  • Head and assistant coaches
  • Athletic directors and support staff
  • Programs that want consistent language across staff and athletes

Common challenges

  • Knowing how to respond to an athlete right after a mistake
  • Communicating clearly under pressure
  • Reinforcing resets without slowing practice
  • Keeping staff and athletes on the same cues

What happens in training

  • An overview of the FRAME Reset Method from the coach's perspective
  • Practice with communication and cue language that fits your sport
  • Scenario work built around real coaching moments
  • A plan for reinforcing resets in practice and competition

Expected takeaways

  • Shared language to support team composure and communication
  • Practical ways to reinforce resets in practice and games
  • A clearer picture of the observable behaviors to coach

Delivery: Format confirmed with you before booking.

05 · Film

Competition-Film Observation and Performance Feedback

Observation of competition film for hesitation, composure, communication, and re-engagement after mistakes, with practical feedback.

Designed for

  • Athletes, coaches, and programs with available competition film
  • Teams that want another set of eyes on how athletes respond after mistakes

Common challenges

  • Not knowing what happens right after a mistake
  • Hesitation before important decisions
  • Changes in body language, communication, or aggression after adversity
  • Difficulty seeing patterns across games

What happens in training

  • Review of available competition film for observable behaviors
  • Identification of habits after mistakes: hesitation, communication, composure, re-engagement, and next-play decisions
  • Practical feedback tied to the FRAME Reset Method
  • Follow-up review to see which behaviors are changing

Expected takeaways

  • A clear, observable picture of post-mistake responses
  • Specific behaviors to train next
  • Feedback coaches and athletes can use in practice

Delivery: Film review runs on your schedule, in person or virtual. Observations describe coachable behaviors, not psychological diagnoses.

06 · Ongoing

Ongoing Athlete-Development Programs

Multi-session development that pairs training with follow-up film review and coach observations across a season or training cycle.

Designed for

  • Athletes and teams who want sustained support, not a one-time session
  • Programs that want to build on an introductory workshop

Common challenges

  • Skills that fade without practice and follow-up
  • Needs that change as the season, role, or level of competition changes
  • Wanting to see what is changing over time

What happens in training

  • A development plan built around the athlete's sport, role, and goals
  • Regular sessions to practice FRAME and pressure scenarios
  • Periodic film and coach-observation reviews
  • Plan updates as behaviors change

Expected takeaways

  • A routine that is practiced, not just discussed
  • Regular feedback on targeted behaviors
  • A clear next step at each stage

Delivery: On your schedule, in person or virtual.
