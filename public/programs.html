WAYS TO WORK TOGETHER

Own the Moment Workshop

Introduce the reset method and rehearse realistic situations as an entry point to live practice and sideline support.

What happens in 30 minutes:

First 5 minutes — Richard meets the team and sets one rule: when the whistle blows, the last play is done.

Next 15 minutes — athletes run through realistic moments — a turnover, a missed assignment, a correction from the coach — and practice the FRAME reset after each one.

Last 10 minutes — one more live rep, then a short coach debrief: what Richard saw, which players reset and which ones got stuck, and what to work on in practice next.
