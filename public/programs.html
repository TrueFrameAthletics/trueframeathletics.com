Own the Moment: Free 30-Minute Team Pilot

Richard comes to your practice and leads the session while you watch. You get a short debrief and one thing to reinforce at practice. There’s no cost or commitment.

  • 5 minutes. Introduce the reset and set a shared rule: when the whistle blows, the last play is done.
  • 15 minutes. Practice the reset after realistic moments like a turnover, missed assignment, or correction.
  • 10 minutes. Run one more live rep, then debrief what Richard saw and what to reinforce next.
