ILLUSTRATIVE BASKETBALL EXAMPLE

What it looks like during a game

A guard commits two turnovers and comes to the bench still replaying the last pass. At a break we’ve agreed on, I ask, “What does Coach need from you when you go back in?” She takes a breath, picks one cue, and comes back with a clear job: get back on defense and call out the matchup.

That’s the FRAME Reset Method in action. The cue isn’t new on game day. We’ve already practiced it, and it reinforces what the staff wants from her on the next possession.
