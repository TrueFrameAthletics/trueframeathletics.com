About TrueFrame

We see the complete player.

Physical skill gets an athlete on the floor. How they handle pressure, mistakes, and setbacks decides what they do once they’re there. TrueFrame exists to develop that second layer: the habits, decisions, and confidence that show up when the game gets hard.

Why we exist

Most development stops at the physical.

Athletes spend thousands of hours on shooting, speed, strength, and technique. Almost none of that time goes to what happens when a game gets hard: the missed free throw, the dropped pass, the turnover, the bad call, the confidence dip, or the moment when the pressure rises.

That’s where talented athletes lose ground. They hesitate. They stop taking the shot. One mistake becomes three. Coaches see it, parents feel it, and the athlete usually can’t explain it clearly.

TrueFrame Athletics treats the mental side of performance as a trainable skill. We use game film to see exactly how an athlete responds under pressure, coach the mental skills that change that response, and build a system that helps the athlete improve over time.

Meet the founder

MEET THE FOUNDER

Richard Cooks founded TrueFrame Athletics to help athletes grow through the moments that challenge them most. Based in Omaha, Richard brings experience coaching young athletes and developing people through feedback, preparation, and clear next steps.

TrueFrame grew from a simple observation: athletes spend hours training their physical skills, but often have no practiced response when confidence drops, a mistake happens, or the pressure rises. Richard built TrueFrame to make that response something athletes can learn, rehearse, and strengthen.

His goal is to help coaches and families see the complete player—and help athletes carry what they learn beyond one game or one season.

Our approach

Mental performance first. Informed by film.

01 · Mental performance

The center of everything we do.

Confidence, composure, resilience, focus, response to mistakes, and performing under pressure. These are skills, and skills can be coached, practiced, and measured.

02 · Film analysis

What the film shows

Decision-making, hesitation, body language, pressure responses, and performance habits in real competition.

03 · Athlete development

What the athlete does next

Specific behaviors, drills, routines, and goals, with measurable improvement over time.
