SIDELINE SUPPORT / PRACTICE INTEGRATION / ALL SPORTS

What I Help Athletes With

Pick a goal that fits your sport. It can be a moment you struggle with or something you want to get better at even when you’re playing well.

Confidence after mistakes and setbacks

Take the next open shot after a miss, shake off a tough call, or rebuild confidence through a slump.

Pressure and game-day nerves

Build a pregame routine and practice a cue you can use when the score is tight or the moment feels bigger than usual.

Focus and composure

Bring your attention back to the next assignment when distractions, frustration, or the pace of play pull it away.

Decision-making

Practice making the next clear read against a press or under a time limit, without rushing or second-guessing the last play.

Playing time and role changes

Stay ready when your minutes change and know your first job when you get back into the game.

Confidence when returning from injury

Once your care team clears you to return, work on confidence and focus as you get back to competition.

General improvement and other goals

Build more consistent communication or preparation habits, or bring a different performance goal you’d like to work on.

This is performance coaching. It does not replace mental health care, injury rehabilitation, or medical clearance.
