For High Schools, Colleges & Club Programs

Own the Moment.

A practical, interactive mental-performance workshop that helps athletes respond to mistakes, stay composed under pressure, communicate through adversity, and prepare for the next opportunity.

What Athletes Practice

F — Find It: Recognize the moment and response.

R — Reset: Interrupt unhelpful thinking or frustration.

A — Anchor: Refocus on a controllable cue or responsibility.

M — Move: Re-engage physically and mentally.

E — Execute: Commit to the next decision and action.

45–60 Minute Workshop Agenda

0–10 MIN

Identify the Moment

Discuss pressure, mistakes, setbacks, and game situations.

10–25 MIN

Learn FRAME

Practice reset cues and mental-performance routines.

25–45 MIN

Pressure Scenarios

Work through realistic, sport-specific situations.

45–60 MIN

Next-Play Plan

Develop individual cues and shared team language.

The workshop is a proposed service format and can be tailored to a team's needs. TrueFrame complements existing coaches, counselors, and sport psychology professionals. Performance coaching is not psychotherapy or clinical treatment.
