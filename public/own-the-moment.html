SIDELINE SUPPORT / PRACTICE INTEGRATION / ALL SPORTS

IN THE MOMENT

An introduction with a place to go next.

Athletes identify a difficult competition moment, rehearse FRAME, and choose a cue tied to their next responsibility. Format and session length are agreed with the program.

01

Find It

Recognize the mistake or pressure moment without letting it define the next one.

02

Reset

Interrupt the reaction with a brief, practiced reset.

03

Anchor

Bring attention to a controllable cue, assignment, or responsibility.

04

Move

Re-engage physically and mentally with the team and competition.

05

Execute

Commit to the next decision and action.

A workshop creates a shared starting point. Practice integration lets athletes rehearse under pressure; approved sideline support helps reinforce those familiar cues during competition.
