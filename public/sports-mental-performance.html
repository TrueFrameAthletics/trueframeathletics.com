Discuss Coaching Support

When one play carries into the next.

Mistakes happen in every sport. The coaching question is what happens afterward: does the athlete regain focus, communicate, and stay connected to the next assignment? TrueFrame focuses on that response while the coaching staff manages the game.

Richard works with the team to establish familiar reset cues in practice. During approved breaks in competition, those cues can support a short, focused check-in with a player who needs help returning attention to the next responsibility.

A reset after back-to-back mistakes.

ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE — NOT A CLIENT RESULT

An athlete comes to the sideline saying, “I can’t do anything right.” Richard first makes room for the coach’s instruction. At an agreed moment, he checks in: “What’s carrying over from that last play? What does Coach need from you when you go back in?”

The athlete uses a practiced FRAME reset and identifies one action, such as communicating the next assignment. Richard watches for that action on the next opportunity. A successful response is something to reinforce; a missed response becomes something to rehearse again.

This example describes the process, not a promise that a brief conversation will change an athlete’s performance.

Bring the response into sports practice.

  • After a mistake: reset and return attention to the next responsibility.
  • Under pressure: use the agreed cue and communicate the next assignment.
  • After correction: identify and rehearse the action requested by the coach.
  • After a substitution: work through frustration and prepare for the next opportunity.

We use situations that fit the team’s existing practice. Staff determine the sport-specific instruction; TrueFrame helps players practice responding to it under pressure.

Support that fits the coaching staff.

Before an engagement, staff and Richard agree on the setting, athlete needs, language, timing, and communication boundaries. A check-in should support the team’s work without competing with a timeout, substitution, or tactical instruction.

Available options include practice integration, approved competition support, and ongoing team support. Individual athlete work can establish a foundation, but it does not automatically grant access to the team bench.

Read how TrueFrame works alongside coaches.

What progress can we look for?

Staff, athlete reflection, and available film can help identify whether reset cues are being used and assignments are being resumed. We look across opportunities rather than treating one play as proof of lasting change.
